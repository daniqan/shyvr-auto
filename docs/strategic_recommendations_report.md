## Risk Management Framework

### Technical Risks and Mitigation Strategies

**High Priority Technical Risks**:

1. **AI Model Performance Degradation** (Probability: 60%, Impact: High)
   - **Risk**: ML models perform poorly in live markets vs. backtesting
   - **Mitigation**: 
     - Implement continuous model validation and retraining
     - Maintain ensemble of models with performance-based weighting
     - Set conservative position sizes during initial deployment
     - Build model fallback systems with traditional technical analysis

2. **System Downtime During Critical Trading** (Probability: 30%, Impact: Very High)
   - **Risk**: Technical failures during high-volatility trading periods
   - **Mitigation**:
     - Implement redundant infrastructure with 99.9% uptime SLA
     - Build automatic failover and circuit breaker systems
     - Maintain emergency stop functionality with manual overrides
     - Provide customer insurance for system failure losses

3. **Data Quality and Market Data Issues** (Probability: 40%, Impact: Medium)
   - **Risk**: Poor data quality leads to incorrect trading decisions
   - **Mitigation**:
     - Use multiple data providers with real-time validation
     - Implement data quality monitoring and alerting systems
     - Build graceful degradation when data feeds are unreliable
     - Maintain historical data backups and reconstruction capabilities

**Ongoing Technical Risk Management**:
- Monthly model performance reviews and optimization
- Quarterly security audits and penetration testing
- Real-time system monitoring with automated alerting
- Regular disaster recovery testing and validation

### Market Risks and Hedging Approaches

**Critical Market Risks**:

1. **Crypto Market Volatility** (Probability: 80%, Impact: High)
   - **Risk**: Extreme volatility makes AI predictions unreliable
   - **Mitigation**:
     - Implement volatility-adjusted position sizing
     - Build regime detection for different market conditions
     - Offer conservative trading modes during high volatility
     - Provide clear risk warnings and education to customers

2. **Regulatory Changes** (Probability: 50%, Impact: Very High)
   - **Risk**: New regulations restrict or prohibit automated trading
   - **Mitigation**:
     - Maintain compliance with current regulations in all jurisdictions
     - Build flexible architecture to adapt to regulatory changes
     - Engage with regulatory bodies and industry associations
     - Consider geographic diversification of operations

3. **Competitive Response** (Probability: 70%, Impact: Medium)
   - **Risk**: Large competitors copy features or undercut pricing
   - **Mitigation**:
     - Focus on differentiated AI capabilities and explainability
     - Build strong customer relationships and community
     - Continuous innovation and research & development
     - Consider patent protection for key innovations

### Financial and Operational Risk Controls

**Financial Risk Management**:

1. **Customer Fund Protection**:
   - Never hold customer funds directly
   - Use established broker custody solutions
   - Implement segregated account structures
   - Provide transparent reporting of all positions and transactions

2. **Position and Risk Limits**:
   - Maximum 2% of portfolio per position
   - Daily loss limits of 5% of account value
   - Maximum leverage of 2:1 for any customer
   - Automatic stop-losses on all positions

3. **Model Risk Controls**:
   - Live trading limits during model validation periods
   - Performance-based model weighting and selection
   - Human oversight for large position changes
   - Regular backtesting and model validation

**Operational Risk Management**:

1. **Key Personnel Risk**:
   - Document all critical processes and systems
   - Cross-train team members on essential functions
   - Maintain contractor relationships for specialized skills
   - Implement succession planning for key roles

2. **Vendor and Dependency Risk**:
   - Maintain multiple data and execution providers

3. **Cybersecurity and Data Protection**:
